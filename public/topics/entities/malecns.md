# MaleCNS

MaleCNS is a wiring map of the adult male fruit fly’s brain and ventral nerve cord. HHMI Janelia, Cambridge, MRC LMB and Google Research collaborated on the project. Version 1.0 was released June 8, 2026; the Cell paper appeared September 3. The dataset is available under CC-BY.

A connectome records neural connections. Simulating behavior also requires choices about neural dynamics, sensory inputs and motor outputs. Game demonstrations should be assessed separately from the validity of the anatomical map.
