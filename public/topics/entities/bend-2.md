# Bend 2

Bend 2 is Victor Taelin's programming language for CPU/GPU execution and proof-checked program properties. Its workflow places requirements in `LAWS.bend`, with implementations and proofs checked by the compiler. A proof concerns the stated property; it does not establish that the specification captures the intended behavior.

Liam Powell's September 18 critique questions the verbosity of the launch demonstration and the ability to redefine functions referenced by laws. Those observations concern that example rather than a benchmark of every Bend program.
