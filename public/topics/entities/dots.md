# Dots

Dots is OpenAI's persistent-agent product introduced at DevDay on September 29, 2026. Powered by GPT-6 Astra, a dot has a cloud computer and can use connected apps to carry out ongoing work. Users can inspect its computer, manage connected access, and review actions under the product's approval controls.

The initial rollout is limited by plan and market. Specialist dots for organizational roles are an enterprise preview; teams of dots are a future plan.
