# Intel

Intel develops processors and accelerators. Its Crescent Island inference GPU architecture uses LPDDR5X rather than HBM: Intel’s Hot Chips 2026 presentation specifies a 160GB branded PCIe card and a design ceiling of 480GB for partner cards, with a 350W air-cooled form factor. These specifications do not establish retail availability or measured workload efficiency.
