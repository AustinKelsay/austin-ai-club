# Sovereign AI Club

## October 21, 2026

Wednesday, 5:30 to 7:30 PM Central (America/Chicago). Free admission.

Bitcoin Park Austin, 601 Congress Avenue, Suite 250, Austin, TX 78701.

Someone will greet you at the main door, and the elevator will be waiting; press 2 for the second floor. If that doesn't work, walk through the Mexta restaurant and take its elevator.

A Socratic discussion of recent AI news and research, with a focus on local AI, open models, and control over the tools we use. Bring a question, share something you have tried, or come to listen.

[Register on Luma](https://luma.com/4v6548j5) · [Bitcoin Park Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/bitcoin-park-austin/events/316963193/)

Topics will be added closer to the meetup. Member projects and questions are welcome in the Community Slot.
