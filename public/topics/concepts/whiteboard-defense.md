# Whiteboard Defense

Mitchell Hashimoto's whiteboard defense is a standard for responsible use of AI in customer-facing software: the person shipping a system should be able to explain its behavior, justify design choices and identify failure modes. It requires architectural understanding rather than recall of every implementation detail. His September 16 post exempts prototypes, demos and experiments.

Ghostty's AI contribution policy is related context, with a different scope: outside contributors must disclose AI use and understand their submitted code; maintainers are exempt.
