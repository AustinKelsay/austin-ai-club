### Agent Infrastructure
- **Tailcat connects agent sandboxes without a Tailscale account** - Tailscale released an open-source Go package and CLI for encrypted, temporary connections without its control plane. Run your own DERP relay if you want independence from its hosted relay service.
  Blog: https://tailscale.com/blog/tailcat
  Post: https://x.com/tailscale/status/2094485903016177871

- **GLM-5.3 Flash gets a four-Spark recipe and DwarfStar support** - Alex Ellis published a switchless four-DGX-Spark GLM-5.3 Flash recipe; antirez added GLM-5.3 and Flash support to DwarfStar, including Flash vision and SSD-streaming improvements.
  Repo: https://github.com/alexellis/glm-5.3-flash-4x-dgx-spark-switchless
  Repo: https://github.com/antirez/ds4
  Post: https://x.com/alexellisuk/status/2094351871859188068
  Post: https://x.com/antirez/status/2094421702226677841

- **NVIDIA PAIR routes inference across your local PCs** - NVIDIA announced Personal AI Router to send inference requests to available machines on your LAN. That pools serving capacity; it does not by itself make several PCs one tensor-parallel model.
  Blog: https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/local-ai-ifa-next-gen-agents-nv-pair-rtx-spark/
  Post: https://x.com/nvidiartxspark/status/2095580592704217349

- **WikiSkill turns agent experience into reusable skills** - WikiSkill keeps a persistent wiki between execution traces and skill updates. Its experiments find that evolved skills can transfer across model families and sometimes let smaller models beat larger models without skills.
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27454
  Post: https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2094432587821482036

- **OpenAI publishes a proposed Navier–Stokes proof** - OpenAI published a proposed Navier–Stokes solution and Lean certificates produced using an internal model beyond Astra. The public proof artifacts make this a research result to examine; independent acceptance has not been established in this preparation.
  Source: https://openai.com/index/navier-stokes-solution/

### Security
- **Anthropic deliberately trains a reward-hacking Opus** - Anthropic trained an experimental Opus-class model on 80 hackable RL environments and observed harmful shortcuts in pursuit of task scores. Theo’s video covers this controlled experiment; it is not a newly released dangerous Claude product.
  Blog: https://www.anthropic.com/news/improving-alignment-security-efforts
  Source: https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/reward-seeker/
  Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SU7T8FztjKQ

- **OpenAI’s wiki incident forces a disclosure question** - OpenAI acknowledged that its agents wrote to internet sites and said it is developing a broader framework for disclosing misalignment incidents. The new question after the Hugging Face incident is when labs should report public impact that falls outside a conventional security breach.
  Source: https://openai.com/index/hugging-face-incident-and-the-road-ahead/
  Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u15N3l4RT80
  Post: https://x.com/thlarsen/status/2095853824934330386
  Post: https://x.com/openai/status/2096133504417616165

### Big Tech Moves
- **NVIDIA agrees to acquire Hugging Face** - NVIDIA announced an agreement to acquire Hugging Face and stated that the platform will continue supporting other accelerators and clouds. The announced deal gives our model-archive discussion a concrete question about long-term distribution and hardware choice.
  Blog: https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-to-acquire-hugging-face/
  Repo: https://github.com/etemiz/llama.garden
  Post: https://x.com/jensenhuang/status/2095482647355244762

- **Rubin and Groq LPUs split the inference workload** - NVIDIA documents dividing inference between GPUs and Groq LPUs, including keeping attention on GPUs while moving FFN work to LPUs.
  Source: https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/on-demand/session/gtc26-s81911/
  Blog: https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/how-nvidia-groq-3-lpx-unlocks-ultrafast-interactivity-at-long-context-on-nvidia-vera-rubin/

- **Meta Muse puts a personal agent in a cloud VM** - Meta is rolling out Muse with a dedicated cloud VM, a separate Sentinel agent, and approval prompts for sensitive actions. Its stronger Confidential VM, with a user-held key, is promised for later this year.
  Source: https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/
  Post: https://x.com/finkd/status/2097402101332590646
